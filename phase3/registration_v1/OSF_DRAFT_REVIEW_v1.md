## Title

Behavioural compatibility among language-model strategies in a mixed iterated prisoner's dilemma tournament: a prospective confirmatory analysis

## Description

This registration specifies the confirmatory analysis of behavioural compatibility among strategies generated by eight language models in a mixed iterated prisoner's dilemma tournament. The eligible archived panel contains 572 strategy identities after 28 exclusions whose missing rules cannot be reconstructed faithfully. Every unordered pair will play one deterministic 1,000-round encounter under each of 16 prespecified interpretation scenarios. The primary directional H7 contrast compares within-model and between-model mutual cooperation, equally weighting 48 model-by-attitude-pair contrasts; significance will be assessed with 10,000 permutations of model labels within attitude (one-sided alpha = 0.05). Self-play is excluded from that contrast. Secondary analyses include AST-duplicate weighting, bounds for excluded identities, all interpretation scenarios, and descriptive payoffs, without additional confirmatory p-values.

The archived strategies, conversion work, and selected semantic, timing, portability, and recovery diagnostics predate this registration, including 31 portability encounters and 11 batch-recovery encounters with real strategies. They are excluded from confirmatory receipts. No complete confirmatory matrix or H7 test has been produced. The frozen plan contains 665 batches; confirmatory execution and analysis will begin only after the public OSF registration is approved and its URL is recorded. Missing or corrupted matches block the matrix and H7 rather than being silently replaced. The complete Spanish protocol, machine configuration, plan context, plan manifest, and SHA-256 inventory are prepared as a small registration attachment. The underlying 1,297 scientific files are locally frozen at Git commit 18cb41744f3f1e66fe711aad09697980476c2498 and are not themselves attached to this registration.

## Summary

This is the Phase 3 confirmatory plan for EvoLLM. It asks whether strategies generated by the same language model cooperate more with one another than with strategies from other models in a mixed iterated prisoner's dilemma. The plan is conditional on an archived eligible panel of 572 strategy identities from eight models. The main Default condition uses deterministic, noise-free 1,000-round encounters. Four adjudicated interpretation choices yield 16 complete scenarios. The exact schedule has 665 batches and 166,172 computationally distinct encounters, with reuse to reconstruct 163,878 logical encounters per scenario. H7 is the single directional confirmatory test: an equally weighted within-model versus between-model mutual-cooperation contrast across 48 model-by-attitude-pair cells, evaluated by 10,000 strategy-label permutations within attitude at one-sided alpha 0.05. All 16 scenarios and the specified sensitivity analyses will be reported.

This registration is prospective to the complete confirmatory tournament and H7 analysis, but it is not a claim that no strategies or outcomes existed before registration. Strategy generation, code conversion, fidelity corrections, semantic and timing checks, and selected real-strategy diagnostics were completed beforehand. Those include 31 portability encounters and 11 batch-recovery encounters. The diagnostics are excluded from the confirmatory receipts and were not used to compute a complete field matrix or H7 statistic. H8, H9, feedback experiments, Prose and Refine conditions, and comparisons with the two previous arXiv preprints are not part of the single confirmatory H7 test.
