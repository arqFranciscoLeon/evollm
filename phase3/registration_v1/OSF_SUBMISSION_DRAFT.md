## Suggested title

Behavioural compatibility among language-model strategies in a mixed iterated prisoner's dilemma tournament: a prospective confirmatory analysis

## English description — short sample for author-voice review

We will examine whether strategies generated by the same language model cooperate more often with one another than with strategies from other models when they meet in a mixed iterated prisoner's dilemma. The eligible archived panel contains 572 identities from eight models, after 28 exclusions whose missing rules cannot be reconstructed faithfully. Each unordered pair will play one deterministic 1,000-round encounter. The primary contrast balances the within-model and between-model difference in mutual cooperation across models and attitude strata; its directional reference distribution uses 10,000 permutations of model labels within attitude. Four adjudicated interpretations define 16 complete scenarios, all of which will be reported. The archived strategies and selected diagnostic encounters predate this registration. No complete confirmatory matrix or H7 test has been produced; those analyses will begin only after the protocol, code, source hashes and 665-batch schedule are registered.

## Information to enter in the registration

- **Study status:** strategies and conversion completed; semantic, timing, portability and batch-recovery diagnostics completed with real strategies. Confirmatory 665-batch execution and H7 analysis have not begun.
- **Question/hypothesis:** one directional H7 for eight *models* (seven laboratories), conditional on this eligible corpus; H8 and H9 are outside the confirmatory study.
- **Design:** 572 Default strategies; 1,000 rounds, one repetition, classic payoffs, no noise; 16 prespecified interpretation masks. 163,878 pairs per mask including self-play, 166,172 distinct computational matches with exact reuse.
- **Primary outcome/analysis:** mutual cooperation CC/1,000; 48 equally weighted model×attitude-pair contrasts; 10,000 strategy-label permutations within attitude, seed 20260913, one-sided α=0.05; self-play excluded.
- **Secondary analyses:** AST-duplicate weighting, algebraic bounds for 28 absent identities, all 16 interpretation masks, descriptive payoffs and attitude mixtures. No additional p-values for these sensitivities.
- **Prior observations:** disclose the 31 portability encounters, 11 batch-recovery encounters and other semantic/calibration diagnostics. They are excluded from confirmatory receipts, and no complete matrix or H7 statistic was computed from the field.
- **Missing/error policy:** a missing, invalid or corrupted match blocks matrix export and H7; no available-case denominator or silent replacement action.
- **Stopping rule:** complete all prespecified matches, subject to technical integrity and an operational credit limit; no result-dependent interim decision.
- **Deviations/amendments:** dated amendments with preserved earlier record; code/source changes invalidate affected matches and require a new context or plan version.
